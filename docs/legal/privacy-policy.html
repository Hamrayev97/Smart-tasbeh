Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026  ·  Also see the Terms of Service

Smart Tasbeh is a dhikr counter, Qibla compass, and prayer-time app. This page explains, in plain terms, what data the app touches, why, and what stays on your device. There is no Smart Tasbeh account and no server database of your personal information — almost everything described below either stays on your phone or is handled by a named third party such as Google.

01 Information we collect

Location

If you open the Qibla or Ramadan screen and grant location permission, the app reads your device's GPS coordinates to calculate the Qibla direction and to fetch prayer times for your area from the Aladhan API (see Third-party services). Your coordinates are sent to Aladhan as part of that one request and are not stored on any server we operate — we don't have one. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time; the app will explain why it's asked and won't request it silently.

Dhikr, statistics, and settings — stored on your device only

Your dhikr counts, daily/weekly/monthly statistics, fasting-tracker entries, and app settings (language, theme, background, notification preferences) are stored locally using on-device storage (SQLite on Android/iOS, browser storage on web). None of this is transmitted to us, because Smart Tasbeh has no backend to send it to. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it permanently.

Advertising identifiers

Unless you have an active Premium subscription, the app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and other technical data to select and measure ads, per Google's own privacy policy — we do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Purchases

The Premium subscription is billed and processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We receive confirmation that a subscription is active so the app can unlock Premium features — we never see your card number, billing address, or other payment details.

Notifications

Prayer-time and dhikr reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using the operating system's local notification scheduler. No notification content or schedule is sent to us or to any third party.

02 How we use this information

  • Calculate the Qibla bearing and fetch accurate daily/monthly prayer times for your location.
  • Track your dhikr counts, streaks, and statistics, entirely on your own device.
  • Show ads to free users, and remove them if you subscribe to Premium.
  • Process and verify your Premium subscription through the app store you installed from.
  • Schedule local reminders for prayer times and daily dhikr, if you turn them on.

We do not use your data for anything beyond delivering the features above.

04 Data sharing & sale

We do not sell your data. We do not have a server that stores your personal information, so there is nothing on our end to share, trade, or hand over beyond the one-off, in-transit location request described above. The only parties that receive any data are the named third-party services in section 03, acting on their own terms.

05 Retention & deletion

Since your dhikr counts, statistics, and settings live only on your device, you are always in control of them: clear the app's storage or uninstall it, and that data is gone. There is no Smart Tasbeh account to close and no copy of your data sitting on a server anywhere, because none exists.

06 Children's privacy

Smart Tasbeh is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we'll address it.

07 Your choices

  • Location: deny or revoke it any time in your device settings — Qibla direction and prayer times just won't be available.
  • Notifications: turn prayer-time and dhikr reminders on or off independently in the app's Settings.
  • Ads: subscribe to Premium to remove them, or continue using the free, ad-supported version.
  • Your data: everything local is deleted by clearing app storage or uninstalling.

08 Security

Requests to third-party services (like Aladhan) use encrypted connections. On-device data is protected by your operating system's own app-sandboxing. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but keeping almost everything on-device — rather than on a server we'd have to defend — is itself a deliberate way we minimize risk.

09 International users

Smart Tasbeh is available in 14 languages and used worldwide. Requests to Aladhan and Google services may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to those providers' own safeguards. We don't otherwise transfer data internationally, since we don't operate a server to transfer it from.
